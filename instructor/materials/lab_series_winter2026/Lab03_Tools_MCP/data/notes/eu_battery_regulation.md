# EU Battery Regulation — study notes

> Synthetic teaching material for Lab 03 (numbers simplified for the exercise — do not cite).

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 on batteries and waste batteries entered into force
  on 17 August 2023 and applies in stages.
- **Recycling efficiency target for lithium-based batteries: 65 % by average weight
  by the end of 2025**, rising to 70 % by the end of 2030.
- Material recovery targets for lithium from waste batteries: 50 % by the end of 2027
  and 80 % by the end of 2031.
- Collection target for waste portable batteries: 63 % by the end of 2027.
- New batteries must carry a battery passport (QR code) from 2027 onwards.
