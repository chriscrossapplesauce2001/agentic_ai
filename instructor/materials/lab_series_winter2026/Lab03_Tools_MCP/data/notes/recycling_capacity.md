# European battery recycling capacity — study notes

> Synthetic teaching material for Lab 03 (all figures invented for the exercise).

- Projected volume of collected lithium-based batteries in Europe for 2026:
  about **500,000 tonnes** (synthetic planning figure used in this lab).
- A typical mid-size recycling plant in this scenario processes 25,000 tonnes per year.
- Pre-treatment (discharge, dismantling, shredding) accounts for roughly 30 % of
  plant operating cost in the scenario; hydrometallurgical recovery for the rest.
